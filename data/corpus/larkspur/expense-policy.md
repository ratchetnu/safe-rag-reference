# Expense Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how Larkspur Instruments employees are reimbursed for business expenses. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees and to contractors with a signed reimbursement addendum. Larkspur Instruments is a fictional company used for demonstration only.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense over $25. Credit card statements are not accepted as a substitute for an itemized receipt. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date; reports submitted later require written approval from your department director.

## Meal limits

The daily meal limit is $75 for domestic travel and $110 for international travel, including tax and tip. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of client entertainment that a director approved in advance.

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under $500 are approved by your direct manager. Expenses from $500 up to $5,000 require approval from the department director. Any expense above $5,000 requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer before the purchase is made.

## Corporate card

The corporate card must not be used for personal purchases, even if you intend to repay them. Cardholders must reconcile their statement in the expense system by the 5th business day of each month.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, gym memberships, seat or cabin upgrades bought for personal comfort, and lost-luggage replacement beyond the airline's settlement.

## Expense report form

All reimbursement requests use form EXP-14 in the expense system. Form EXP-14 records the business purpose, cost center and receipts for each line item.
