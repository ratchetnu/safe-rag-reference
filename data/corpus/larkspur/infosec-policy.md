# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the VPN and every system reachable from outside the office network.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. USB mass storage devices are blocked on company laptops; request an exception from the security team if you need one for a specific task.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Report suspected phishing within 1 hour using the Report Phish button in the mail client. Do not forward the suspicious message to colleagues. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, also call the IT service desk immediately.

## Data classification

Larkspur Instruments uses four data classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Patching

Critical security patches must be installed within 7 days of release. High-severity patches must be installed within 30 days.
