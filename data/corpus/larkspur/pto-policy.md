# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at 1.75 days per month, which is 21 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours. PTO covers vacation and personal time.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31. Unused days above the carryover limit are forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting time off

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance. Shorter requests need at least 2 business days of notice. Managers respond to requests within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 8 sick days per year, and a doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member.

## Company holidays

Larkspur Instruments observes 11 paid company holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published every November for the following year.
