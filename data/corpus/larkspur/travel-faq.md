# Travel FAQ

## Hotel limits

The hotel limit is $220 per night. In designated high-cost cities the hotel limit is $300 per night. Stays above the limit require a written justification attached to the expense report.

## Who books my travel?

You book your own travel through the company travel portal. Travel coordinators can help with complex multi-city trips.
