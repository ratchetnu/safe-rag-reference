# Travel Policy

## Booking

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal so that trips are covered by travel insurance and duty-of-care tracking.

## Air travel

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Lodging

The hotel limit is $220 per night. In designated high-cost cities the hotel limit is $300 per night. Stays above the limit require a written justification attached to the expense report.

## Ground transportation and mileage

Personal car use is reimbursed at $0.65 per mile. Rideshare and taxis are allowed for business travel; rental cars should be compact or midsize.

## International travel

International trips must be approved at least 21 days before departure. Travelers must register the trip with the security team for destination risk briefings.
