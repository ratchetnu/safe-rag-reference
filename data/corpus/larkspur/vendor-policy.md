# Vendor Management Policy

## Security review

A new vendor must complete security questionnaire VSQ-3 before onboarding if it will process Internal, Confidential or Restricted data. The security team reviews VSQ-3 responses within 10 business days.

## Competitive bids

Purchases over $25,000 require three competitive bids. Purchases over $50,000 also require a contract review by the legal team.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees involved in selecting a vendor must disclose any personal or financial relationship with that vendor before the selection begins.

## Payment terms

Standard vendor payment terms are Net 45. Shorter payment terms require approval from the finance team.

## Vendor expenses

Vendor invoices are not expense reports. Vendor invoices are paid through accounts payable after a purchase order is approved, and they must never be submitted on form EXP-14.
