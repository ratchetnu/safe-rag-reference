# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Scope

This policy covers business expenses incurred by Quillfeather Logistics staff. Quillfeather Logistics is a fictional company used for demonstration only.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any expense over $40. Expense reports must be filed within 45 days of the expense date.

## Meal limits

The daily meal limit is $60 for domestic travel and $95 for international travel.

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under $1,000 are approved by your manager. Expenses from $1,000 up to $10,000 require approval from a Vice President (VP). Expenses above $10,000 require approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

## Expense report form

Reimbursement requests are filed on form QL-EXP-2. Form QL-EXP-2 must include the shipment or project code the expense relates to.
