# Security Standards

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Hardware security keys are required for administrator accounts and for remote access.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Report suspected phishing to the security mailbox within 30 minutes of noticing it.

## Patching

Critical security patches must be installed within 72 hours of release.

## Removable media

USB storage is allowed only on encrypted, company-issued drives.
