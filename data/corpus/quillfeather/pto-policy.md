# Time Off Policy

## Accrual

Staff accrue paid time off (PTO) at 1.5 days per month, which is 18 days per year.

## Carryover

Up to 10 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next year.

## Requesting time off

Submit time-off requests at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than one week.

## Sick leave

Staff receive 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO.

## Volunteer day

Each employee receives 1 paid volunteer day per year for community service.
