# Business Travel Policy

## Air travel

Economy class is standard. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 8 hours.

## Lodging

The hotel limit is $180 per night in all cities.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at $0.60 per mile.

## International travel

International trips must be approved at least 14 days before departure.
