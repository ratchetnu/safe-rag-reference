# Supplier Policy

## Risk assessment

New suppliers complete the QF-VRA vendor risk assessment before their first purchase order.

## Competitive bids

Purchases over $15,000 require at least two competitive bids.

## Payment terms

Standard supplier payment terms are Net 30.
